Three ways to help

You don’t have to run a position to help. Pick the level that fits your life.

Hold it

Take the position and own it. You’re the person the committee turns to for that job.Take the position and own it.

Back it up

Learn a key job next to the person who holds it, and cover when they can’t.Learn the job and cover when the holder can’t.

Support it

Help with one piece of it, like a trip, a fundraiser or one badge. No title needed.Help with one piece of it. No title needed.

Scouting America suggests about an hour a week, but the position is what you make of it. Limited time? We’ll work with that.

Merit badges we offer

Badges a Troop 54 counselor can teach.

How to start a merit badge

  1. 1

    Pick a badge you want to earn.

  2. 2

    Talk to the Scoutmaster. They’ll sign your blue card and give you a counselor’s contact.

  3. 3

    Reach out to the counselor with a parent or another adult copied on every message.

  4. 4

    Meet, finish the requirements, then bring your signed card back to the Scoutmaster.
