Food Drive Leader Guide

For the Scouts, families and leaders running the Rotterdam Scouts Food Drive: fliers on Saturday, November 7, and pickup, drop-off and sorting on Sunday, November 15.

To confirm marks a detail that isn't final yet. This page will be updated as each one is set.

Who Does What

  • Event lead: Will Mau. Works with the Rotterdam Community Center and answers questions.
  • Saturday fliers: the Cubmaster and Pack 3054 den leaders, with Cub Scout families.
  • Sunday routes: the Scoutmaster, with Troop 3054 Scouts and adult drivers.
  • At the RCC: RCC staff open the building and show us where things go. A sort lead runs the tables To confirm.
  • Greeters: a Scout and an adult at the drop-off, thanking people and taking donations from their cars.

Saturday, November 7: Fliers

  1. Get your bundle of fliers and your street list To confirm.
  2. One flier per house. Never put a flier in or on a mailbox; that's against federal law. Tuck it in the door handle or wedge it in the frame.
  3. Skip houses with a "No Soliciting" sign.
  4. Stay on sidewalks and driveways, cross streets together, and finish in daylight.
  5. Tell the event lead which streets you covered. Sunday's pickup routes are built from that list, so every house with a flier gets a visit.

Cub Scouts stay with their own adult the whole time. Lions and Tigers always work with their adult partner.

Sunday, November 15: Pickup Routes

  1. Meet at To confirm before 9 AM. Each car gets a route, a tally sheet and spare bags.
  2. Each car is an adult driver with two or more Scouts, or a parent with their own child.
  3. Drive the route slowly with your hazard lights on. Anyone out of the car wears a safety vest.
  4. Pick up bags at the end of driveways only. Don't walk up to houses or ring doorbells.
  5. Double-bag anything torn or wet, and keep a tally of bags as you go.
  6. When the car is full or the route is done, drive to the RCC, unload, and head back out if streets are left.

Safeguarding Youth: no adult is ever alone with a Scout who isn't their own child, and that includes in a car.

Sunday, November 15: Drop-Off and Sorting at the RCC

Rotterdam Community Center, 705 Curry Rd. Open 10 AM to 1 PM To confirm.

  1. Sign in with your name, unit and arrival time, so everyone's service hours count.
  2. Greet and unload. Scouts take donations from cars at the drop-off spot To confirm and carry them to the sort tables.
  3. Sort by type, the way the RCC asked: pasta and rice, canned beans, canned fruit, canned vegetables, soups, peanut butter, cereal and oatmeal, and everything else.
  4. Blessing Box bin: set aside glass jars and a mix of other items for Christ's Church of the Hills. They're picked up the week of November 16.
  5. Check dates: anything opened or expired goes in its own box for RCC staff to decide.
  6. Count: tally the bags, or weigh the boxes, so we can share a total with the RCC and our families.

Photos: the RCC may take pictures for a press release. Only photograph Scouts whose parents have said yes.

What to Bring and Wear

  • A Scout shirt or unit T-shirt, layers for the weather, and gloves.
  • Water and a snack.
  • Drivers: an empty trunk or back seat and a charged phone.

Supplies

Fliers, street lists, tally sheets, safety vests, spare bags, box labels and markers, tape, boxes or bins for sorting (ask the RCC what they have), and a scale if we're weighing.

After the Drive

  • Send your tally to the event lead.
  • Log service hours: Troop 3054 families in the troop app, Pack 3054 families in Scoutbook. Hours count toward Scouting America's Impact365.
  • Scouts who take part can wear the Impact365 National Day of Service emblem.
