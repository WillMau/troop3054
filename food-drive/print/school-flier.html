Hi, Schalmont families! Scouts from Troop 3054 and Pack 3054, who meet at Jefferson Elementary, are collecting food for the Rotterdam Community Center's food pantries. Pull up and Scouts will take it right from your car. A bag, a box or a single can all help.

Make it a family thing: let your kids pick out the food and hand it to a Scout.

Most needed

Canned fruit & veggies
Beans, any kind
Rice, noodles & mac and cheese
Shelf-stable milk
Lunchbox snacks
Hot cocoa & tea

Sloppy joe sauce is on their list too, and any shelf-stable food helps. Unopened and in date, please. Nothing that needs a fridge or freezer, and no alcohol.

Got a Scout flier on your door?

Then you can skip the trip. Leave your bag at the end of your driveway by 7 AM Sunday, and Troop 3054 Scouts will pick it up.
