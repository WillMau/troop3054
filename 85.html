85 Years. Since 1941.

For 85 years, Troop 54 has called Rotterdam home — taking local kids camping, teaching them to lead, and sending them off with stories they still tell decades later. As we celebrate our 85th year, we're gathering our history, and we need your help.

Were you a Scout in Troop 54 (now Troop 3054)? A parent, a leader, an Eagle? We'd love:

📸 Old photos (campouts, Courts of Honor, that haircut from 1987)
📖 Your stories and memories
🏅 Scoutmasters and Eagle Scouts we might be missing
🎽 Old patches, handbooks, or uniforms gathering dust

See our Eagle Scouts through the years on our Eagle Scout page, and help us fill in the names we've lost track of. Have photos? Email them to info@troop3054.org.

Share your memory

Whether you crossed over in 1955 or 2005, you're part of this story. Here's to the next 85 years.
