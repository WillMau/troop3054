Save the date: Food Drive

Hi everyone,

For years our Scouts have collected food every November for neighbors who need it. This year we're keeping it close to home. Troop 3054 and Pack 3054 are teaming up with the Rotterdam Community Center (RCC) on a food drive for Rotterdam residents, and everything we collect goes to the RCC to help families right here in town heading into the holidays.

We're still working out the details, but please hold these dates.

Part of the National Day of Service

Scouting America is asking Scouts and families across the country to serve their communities on Saturday, November 14, with food drives leading the way. Many of our Scouts will be at the GE merit badge day that Saturday, so our drive surrounds it: fliers the Saturday before and pickup the day after.

Hours from the drive get logged in Scoutbook and count toward Impact365, Scouting America's year-round service effort. Scouts who take part can wear the Impact365 National Day of Service emblem, sold at Scout Shops and online. Learn more

How you can help
Cub Scout families. Help hand out fliers on Saturday 11/7, and bring your Cub to the RCC on the 15th to help sort if you can.
Scout families. Hold Sunday 11/15 for pickup and sorting. We'll need adult drivers for the pickup routes.
Everyone. Tell your neighbors, and fill a bag yourself. Everything collected stays in Rotterdam.
What to give

Canned beans
Canned fruit & veggies
Soups

Can't make Sunday? The RCC also takes drop-offs weekdays from 9 AM to 5 PM at 705 Curry Rd, Rotterdam.

We'll share times, neighborhoods and the flier itself as soon as they're set. For now, please hold the dates. This is a wonderful chance for our Scouts to help their own neighbors right before the holidays.
