Hi everyone,

Thank you to every Scout and grown-up who came out on Saturday. This was a real joint effort between Troop 3054, Pack 3054 and Girl Scout Troop 5152, and together we put a flier on 1,637 doors across Rotterdam. That's about 32 hours of walking, all 25 routes, in one morning.

A special thank you to Girl Scout Troop 5152 for joining us. It's great to see Scouts from all three units working side by side for our neighbors.

NEXT WEEK · SUNDAY, NOVEMBER 15 · RAIN OR SHINE
Now we collect the food
By 7 AMHomes that got a flier set a bag of food at the end of the driveway.
MorningTroop 3054 drives the routes and picks up every bag.
10 AM to 2 PMWe're at the Rotterdam Community Center, 705 Curry Rd, taking drop-offs from neighbors, then sorting, counting and stocking the pantry shelves.
We still need help
  • Drivers and troop Scouts for the pickup routes. Each car is an adult driver with two or more Scouts, or a parent with their own child.
  • Helpers at the RCC from all three units to greet cars, carry donations in, sort and count. Even an hour helps.
  • Troop families, please RSVP in the troop app. Everyone else, just reply and let us know when you can come.
WHY IT MATTERS
It all stays right here in our community
Everything we collect goes to the Rotterdam Community Center's food pantries, which serve more than 1,000 of our neighbors every month. That includes their new mobile pantry, which sends food home with Mohonasen families after school. Every bag on a driveway next Sunday is food on a neighbor's table.

Help spread the word. Not every home got a flier, so please share the Facebook event with friends and neighbors. Anyone can drop off food at the RCC on Sunday from 10 AM to 2 PM.

And don't forget to log Saturday's service hours.
