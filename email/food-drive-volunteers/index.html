Troop 3054 · Pack 3054 · Rotterdam, NY
Rotterdam Scouts Food Drive
Fliers go out Saturday, 11/7. Food comes in Sunday, 11/15. We need Scouts and parents for both.

Hi everyone,

Troop 3054 and Pack 3054 are collecting food for the Rotterdam Community Center's food pantries, which serve more than 1,000 of our neighbors every month. It runs over two weekends, and we can't do it without you.

Here's what each day looks like and where we need help.

SATURDAY, NOVEMBER 7 · 10 AM · JEFFERSON ELEMENTARY
Flier day: get every flier on a door

Meet us at Jefferson Elementary, 100 Princetown Rd, at 10 AM. We'll hand each family a stack of fliers and a map of their route.

There are 25 routes, and each one takes about an hour on foot. That's the whole goal for Saturday: get the fliers out. We need a family on every route, so please come if you can, and take a second route if you have the time.

Good to know
  • Your Scout stays with you the whole time. Lions and Tigers walk with their adult partner.
  • One flier per house, tucked in the door. Never in or on the mailbox. Postal rules don't allow it.
  • Skip houses with a "No Soliciting" sign, and skip apartments.
  • Stay on sidewalks and driveways, and cross streets together.
  • When you finish, let Kyle or Jackie Stark know, and grab another route if one's still open.
  • Troop families: Saturday is also orienteering at Rotary, so this day is mostly the pack. If you're around, come walk a route with us.
SUNDAY, NOVEMBER 15 · RAIN OR SHINE
Pickup, drop-off and sorting
By 7 AMHomes that got our flier leave a bag of food at the end of the driveway.
8 to 9 AMTroop 3054 picks up all of the food. Pickup cars leave the Jefferson Elementary parking lot, each with a route.
10 AM to 2 PMWe're stationed at the Rotterdam Community Center, 705 Curry Rd, taking donations from neighbors who drive up, and sorting everything that comes in.
Around noonAn RCC volunteer shows us where everything goes, and we start stocking the pantry shelves.
After 2We count every item, put it all away inside the RCC, and wrap up.
Who we need
  • Troop Scouts and adult drivers for the pickup routes. Each car is an adult driver with two or more Scouts, or a parent with their own child. Drivers, bring a few extra grocery bags.
  • Troop and pack helpers at the RCC to greet cars, carry donations in, sort, and count. Cub Scouts are welcome with a parent, even for an hour.
  • Safeguarding Youth: no adult is ever alone with a Scout who isn't their own child, and that includes in a car.
MOST NEEDED AT THE PANTRY
Canned fruit and veggies · Beans, any kind · Rice, noodles and mac and cheese · Shelf-stable milk · Lunchbox snacks · Hot cocoa and tea
Unopened and in date, please, and nothing that needs a fridge or freezer. We don't collect money, so kindly turn down cash or checks.

Help spread the word. Share the Facebook event and the food drive page with friends and neighbors. Anyone who doesn't get a flier can still drop off food at the RCC on Sunday from 10 AM to 2 PM.

Both days count as service hours, so keep track of how long you help.
