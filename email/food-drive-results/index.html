Hi everyone,

Thank you to every Scout, parent and leader who took part in the Rotterdam Scouts Food Drive. We could not have done it without your help.

I loved watching the Cub Scouts work side by side with the older Scouts to help our neighbors in need. This is what Scouting is all about. Together you collected 2,059 items to help feed our most vulnerable neighbors in this time of need.

A special thank you to Girl Scout Troop 5152 for joining us on flier day and driving routes this morning.

HOW WE DID IT
Two days, three Scout units
Sat, Nov 7Cub Scouts and Girl Scouts walked every route and left a flier at 1,637 homes.
Sun, Nov 15Families drove all 25 routes to pick up bags, then we sorted and counted everything at the Rotterdam Community Center.
WHERE IT'S GOING
It all stays right here in our community
Most of it goes to the Rotterdam Community Center's food pantries, which serve more than 1,000 of our neighbors every month. About 100 items go to the Blessing Box at Christ's Church of the Hills in Mariaville.
Thank you to the Rotterdam Community Center and Bread of Life for partnering with us and opening their doors to our Scouts.

Scouts, don't forget to log your service hours from flier day and pickup day.

Thank you all again!

Will Mau
