Hi everyone,

Quick reminder that tomorrow, Saturday, 11/7, is flier day for the Rotterdam Scouts Food Drive. We're meeting in the Jefferson Elementary parking lot, 100 Princetown Rd, at 10 AM to hand out the fliers. Then each family takes a route and leaves a flier at every house on it.

The route maps are attached. Each page is one route, with a dot on every house that gets a flier. Take a look tonight if you'd like to pick one, or choose when you get there.

TOMORROW · SATURDAY, NOVEMBER 7
How the morning works
10 AMMeet in the Jefferson Elementary parking lot. Pick a route and grab a stack of fliers.
ThenWalk your route and leave a flier at every house on your map. Most routes take about an hour.
When you're doneLet Kyle or Jackie Stark know, and take a second route if one's still open.
Good to know
  • Your Scout stays with you the whole time. Lions and Tigers walk with their adult partner.
  • One flier per house, tucked in the door. Never in or on the mailbox.
  • Skip houses with a "No Soliciting" sign, and skip apartments.
  • Stay on sidewalks and driveways, and cross streets together.
  • Dress for the weather and wear comfortable shoes.
THEN, SUNDAY, NOVEMBER 15
We collect the food
Homes that got a flier set a bag of food at the end of the driveway, and Troop 3054 drives the routes to collect it. We're also at the Rotterdam Community Center, 705 Curry Rd, from 10 AM to 2 PM, taking drop-offs from neighbors, then sorting and counting everything. Troop and pack helpers are welcome.
Troop families: please RSVP for Sunday in the troop app.
