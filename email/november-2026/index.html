November with the troop
Special call out · Sunday, November 15
Rotterdam Scouts Food Drive
We need adult drivers. Please RSVP in the app either way.

Hi everyone,

November's a full month. The big one is Sunday, 11/15, when we drive around Rotterdam picking up food for the Rotterdam Community Center's food pantries. Pack 3054's Cub Scouts hand out the fliers on Saturday, 11/7, and on Sunday the homes that got one leave a bag of food at the end of their driveway for us to pick up.

A couple of things changed since my last email: bags go at the end of the driveway, not on the porch, and routes start between 8 and 9 AM.

Then the weekend of the 20th is our troop trip to Camp Wakpominee, built around rank advancement and cooking. Please RSVP for that one by Monday, 11/9.

SUNDAY, NOVEMBER 15 · RAIN OR SHINE
How the day runs
8 to 9 AMPickup routes leave the Jefferson Elementary parking lot. Each car gets a route.
10 AMBring everything to the Rotterdam Community Center, 705 Curry Rd. We sort in the lobby while neighbors drop off food too.
Around noonAn RCC volunteer shows us how to stock the pantry shelves downstairs.
After 2We tally it all up together and wrap up.
Drivers, a few things
  • Each car is an adult driver with two or more Scouts, or a parent with their own child.
  • Only pick up bags at the end of driveways. No bag? Keep driving. Don't walk up to houses, knock or ring doorbells.
  • Grab a few extra grocery bags from home, and dress for the weather.
Safeguarding Youth: no adult is ever alone with a Scout who isn't their own child, and that includes in a car.
We'll hand out routes and go over safety at the troop meeting on Monday, 11/9.

Troop trip to Camp Wakpominee
A weekend built around rank advancement and cooking

We meet at camp at 5 PM on Friday, 11/20, and head home Sunday around 3 PM. Scouts cook every meal. We build the weekend around the Scouts who RSVP, so the sooner we know, the better it fits what your Scout needs.

NEWER SCOUTS
Trail to First Class
Tent camping, patrol cooking, knots and lashing, first aid, and a 5-mile map-and-compass hike Sunday morning, depending on what your Scout still needs.
FINISHED FIRST CLASS COOKING?
Cooking merit badge
Scouts can finish the whole badge by the end of the campout. Get the Scoutmaster's OK, start the five-day activity log now, and bring the packet to Badge Day. Parents, please supervise and sign the home cooking log.
Parents: we still need drivers Friday and Sunday, and registered adults to stay at camp. Reply to this email if you can help.
