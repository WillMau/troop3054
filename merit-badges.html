Troop class · Meeting, home and campout

Cooking

Required for Eagle

Scouts learn the classroom requirements at a troop meeting, plan and cook at home, then cook with their crews on a campout. Every crew plans its meals from the same baskets on a set budget per person, and the adults they feed count too.

At a meeting
Badge Day: health and safety, nutrition and cooking basics (requirements 1 to 3), and day 1 of the 5-day log.
At home
The 5-day log and meal plan, requirement 7, and a home meal plan the counselor approves before four meals get cooked.
Before camp
Camp and trail menus from the meal baskets. Each crew picks its menus and the counselor reviews them.
On the campout
Crew cooking, cleanup and Leave No Trace, plus trail meals on a hike.

Workshop · About 4 hours

Artificial Intelligence

A hands-on workshop led by a counselor who works in tech. Scouts play AI or Not?, talk through real ethics scenarios, learn to spot deepfakes, practice writing prompts and interview a professional about AI careers.

Before
Research AI ethics (bias, privacy, and AI making decisions about people) to share in the workshop.
In the workshop
Requirements 1 to 6 and 8, with a break halfway.
After
Requirement 7, a project or a lesson you teach, if there wasn’t time for the teachback.

Badges our counselors teach

Any Troop 3054 Scout can earn these with one of our own counselors.

How to start a merit badge

  1. 1

    Pick a badge you want to earn.

  2. 2

    Talk to the Scoutmaster. They’ll sign your blue card and give you a counselor’s contact.

  3. 3

    Reach out to the counselor with a parent or another adult copied on every message.

  4. 4

    Meet, finish the requirements, then bring your signed card back to the Scoutmaster.

We don’t post counselor names online. The Scoutmaster connects each Scout with a counselor.
